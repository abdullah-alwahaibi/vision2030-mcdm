AHP consistency checks

A consistency ratio below 0.10 means the pairwise judgements are acceptably consistent.

CI = (λmax − n) / (n − 1) and CR = CI / RI, with RI = 1.12 for n = 5, 0.90 for n = 4 and 0.58 for n = 3. A 2×2 matrix is always perfectly consistent, so its CR is 0 by definition.

    2. Do the six scenarios agree?

    Kendall rank correlation (τ-b) between every pair of scenarios. 1 means identical order; lower means the choice of method matters more.

    3. VIKOR validity conditions

    VIKOR only names a single best project when two conditions hold. C1 (acceptable advantage): the gap in Q between first and second place is at least 1 / (J − 1), here 1/11 = 0.091. C2 (acceptable stability): the leader is also first by S or by R. If C1 fails, the best answer is a compromise set rather than one winner.

    4. Sensitivity to v in VIKOR

    v is the weight on group utility (S) versus individual regret (R). The main analysis uses 0.5; AHP weights here. Orange ranks differ from v = 0.5.
